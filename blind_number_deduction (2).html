Blind Number Deduction — Official Rules

1. Secret Numbers & Starting Hands

Each player receives a unique hidden whole number from 1 to 100—you cannot see your own number or anyone else's. Every player starts with 4 option cards: Add, Multiply, Divide, and Zero.

2. Pairing Up & Spending Cards

Two players agree to pair up and play one or more shared card types together. Both players spend 1 copy of each played card. Playing k cards in one deal advances the round counter by k rounds.

  • Add (+): Exact sum of both numbers (A + B).
  • Multiply (×): Last digit only of their product ((A × B) mod 10).
  • Divide (÷): Integer quotient of the larger number divided by the smaller (⌊max(A,B) ÷ min(A,B)⌋).
  • Zero (0): How many multiples of 10 (10, 20, …, 90) lie strictly between the two numbers (excluding the players' own numbers if they end in 0).

3. Buying Extra Card Sets

Any player may buy a fresh set of all 4 cards at any time (no limit, does not spend a round) at a permanent cost of -1 point at the end of the game.

4. Game End & Scoring

The game ends after 4 × Players + 2 rounds, when no playable pairs remain, or when players vote to end early for final guessing:

  • Your Own Number: Correct = +3 pts | Incorrect = -3 pts | Blank = 0 pts.
  • Another Player's Number: Correct = +2 pts to you and -2 pts to them | Incorrect = -2 pts to you | Blank = 0 pts.
