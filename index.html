Blind Number Deduction — Rules & Privacy

1. Secret Numbers & Starting Cards

Each player is assigned a unique hidden whole number from 1 to 100—you cannot see your own number or anyone else's. Every player begins with 4 operator cards: Add, Multiply, Divide, and Zero.

2. Strict Pair-Only Information Privacy

When two players agree to pair up and spend 1 or more shared cards, ONLY those two participating players learn which cards were played and what the numerical outputs are. All other players only see that those two players completed a private exchange of k cards (advancing the clock by k rounds).

3. Operator Card Formulas

  • Add (+): Exact sum A + B.
  • Multiply (×): Last digit only of A × B.
  • Divide (÷): Integer quotient ⌊max(A,B) ÷ min(A,B)⌋.
  • Zero (0): Count of multiples of 10 (10, 20, …, 90) strictly between A and B.

4. Extra Sets, Game End & Scoring

Buying a fresh set of 4 cards costs -1 pt (no round cost). The match ends after 4N + 2 rounds, when no valid pairs remain, or when players end for final guesses. Once guesses lock in, all secret numbers and private clues are declassified:

  • Own Number: Exact = +3 pts | Wrong = -3 pts | Blank = 0 pts.
  • Opponent Number: Exact = +2 pts to you & -2 pts to them | Wrong = -2 pts to you | Blank = 0 pts.
